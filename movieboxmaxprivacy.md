Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026
Overview

Brave Studio("BoxMax," "we," "us"), a company registered in the UK, respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you use BoxMax and how we use it.
Information we collect

We aim to collect as little as possible. We may collect basic account details you choose to provide (such as an email for your bookings), and anonymous usage and diagnostic data that helps us keep the app fast and reliable.
How we use information

Information is used to operate and improve the app — showing showtimes near you, processing and delivering your ticket bookings, fixing bugs, and understanding which features are useful. We do not sell your personal information.
Third-party data

BoxMax uses data from The Movie Database (TMDB) to display movie details such as titles, descriptions, posters, ratings, and cast information. This data is provided by TMDB and is subject to their own terms of use and privacy policy. BoxMax does not share your personal information with TMDB. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.
Advertising & tracking

BoxMax contains no advertising. We do not display ads, and we do not include third-party advertising or tracking SDKs. We do not sell or share your personal information with advertisers, and we do not use your data for ad targeting.
Your choices

You can use most of BoxMax without an account. You may request deletion of any account data at any time by contacting us.
